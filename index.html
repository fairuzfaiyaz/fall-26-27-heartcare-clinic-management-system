About HeartCare

Healthcare Made More Connected

HeartCare is a clinic management platform designed to make the patient journey more organized — from booking an appointment to reviewing consultation notes, laboratory reports, and invoices.

Patients, doctors, laboratory technicians, and administrators use role-specific areas connected through one system.

  • Convenient appointment management
  • Organized consultation and laboratory records
  • Patient access to reports and billing information
