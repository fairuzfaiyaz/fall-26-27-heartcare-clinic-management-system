## Seed-data policy

The arrays are useful as synthetic demo material only after reconciliation. Do not copy every array as independent seed rows. The unresolved identities, department/test catalogues, appointment conflicts, invoice amounts, and lab result references must be resolved first. Reconciliation should produce one shared user/patient/doctor/technician/test set, then linked appointments, consultations, prescriptions, lab orders/results, invoices, and payments.
